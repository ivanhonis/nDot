Introduction:

nDot.io is a development project.Our team current goal is to develop a framework, based on proprietary tools and applications, to execute AI-based, quantitative trade strategies without any limitations.

Research Areas of Interest:

  • Classification of time series data from the stock market

    Classification of historical stock market data based on neural network training, in order to predict future outcomes
  • Boost prediction with analysis of stock market orderbook

    Develop trade strategies, employing deep neural networks, to uncover/predict profitable trades
  • Arbitrage Opportunities on the Cryptomarkets

    Analyzing and executing arbitrage opportunities on cryptocurrency markets
